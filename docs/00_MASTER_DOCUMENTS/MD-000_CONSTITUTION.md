> Private Office is an operating system for administering estate, companies, investments, and financial decisions.

## Mission

Centralize the group's economic, financial, estate, and documentary knowledge in one trusted and intelligent ecosystem, so important decisions can be made with complete, timely, and explainable information.

## Constitutional principles

1. **One source of truth.** A fact is mastered once, then referenced; duplicate records are defects.
2. **Every economic object has an owner.** Persons, legal entities, projects, and other legal vehicles may own value, rights, and obligations.
3. **Evidence and lineage are mandatory.** Financial changes preserve their source, approval, author, time, and reversal chain.
4. **AI proposes; authorized humans decide.** AI cannot autonomously alter financial, legal, ownership, or payment records.
5. **The experience is not an ERP.** Accounting is a controlled internal capability, while the user experience is a calm decision workspace.
6. **Simplicity is a requirement.** Context precedes configuration; a user should never need to assemble a report to understand today's priorities.
7. **Documents have context.** Files must be related to an accountable entity and may have several explicit relationships.
8. **The group is relational.** Companies, projects, investments, and people must support evolving ownership and financing relationships.
9. **Estate is the outcome; accounting is a representation.** Formal books are indispensable but do not replace economic understanding.
10. **Technology is replaceable; the domain is not.** Integrations, models, databases, and accounting engines may evolve without losing identity or history.

## What Private Office is and is not

It is a private operating system for a growing business group and personal estate. It is not SAP, Odoo, ERPNext, a spreadsheet, a CRM, or a standalone accounting package. Those can be integrated services or bounded capabilities.

## Constitutional rules

- New functionality must identify the owner, evidence, timeline events, permissions, and audit behavior of its records.
- Financial records are corrected through controlled reversals or adjustments; they are not silently overwritten.
- The system supports multiple entities, currencies, countries, users, and future companies from inception.
- A change to these principles requires an approved ADR and a major version change to this document.
